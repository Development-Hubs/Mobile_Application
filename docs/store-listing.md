**Full description:**
EN: OneSource Connect is the companion app of the OneSource ERP used by manpower companies in Qatar.
Staff check in and out, take breaks, ask for attendance corrections, handle help-desk cases, renewals, care calls,
accommodation, trips and training. Customers follow recruitment, choose a candidate or book a video interview,
read and sign agreements, see invoices and receipts, and ask for help. Workers see their documents, accommodation,
trips, salary statements, training and certificates. Available in English and Arabic.
An invitation or an account from your company is required.

## Data safety / App privacy answers

| Data | Collected | Why | Shared |
|---|---|---|---|
| Name, email, phone | Yes (account) | sign-in, account | No |
| Precise location | Only when the company requires it at check-in, and at trip milestones | attendance, trips | No |
| Photos and files | Only when the person attaches them | help desk, trip proof, assessment evidence | No |
| Signature image | Only when the customer signs an agreement | contract record | No |
| Device id / push token | Yes, if push is enabled | notifications | Firebase (processor) |
| Face data | **No** — biometric unlock is done by the phone; the app receives yes/no only | — | — |

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Users can sign out devices and ask the company to delete their account.
A privacy policy URL from your company's website is required by both stores.
