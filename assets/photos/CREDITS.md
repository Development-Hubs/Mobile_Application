# Photographs

`doha.jpg` is downloaded on the build machine by `tool/run_windows.ps1` (or `tool/fetch_photos.sh`) from
Wikimedia Commons, because it is not ours to commit without its licence notice:

* **File:** [DohaSkylineAtNight.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:DohaSkylineAtNight.jpg) — skyline of Doha near the Corniche at night
* **Licence:** Creative Commons Attribution 2.0 (CC BY 2.0), originally published on Flickr
* **Credit shown in the app:** Settings → About → "Photo: Doha skyline at night, Wikimedia Commons, CC BY 2.0"

Before a store release, replace it with the company's own photograph (or a licensed one) of the same name,
at least 1080 × 1600 pixels. If the file is absent, the app falls back to its drawn West Bay skyline.
